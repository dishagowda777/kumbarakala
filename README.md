# Kumbara-Kala 🏺

### Digital Marketplace for Traditional Pottery Artisans

Kumbara-Kala is a bilingual Android application developed using **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose** to support traditional pottery artisans by providing a digital platform to showcase and sell handmade clay products. The application promotes cultural heritage, eco-friendly living, and direct artisan-to-customer interaction.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The main objective of Kumbara-Kala is to empower rural pottery artisans by helping them digitally showcase their products and connect directly with customers without intermediaries. The app also promotes sustainable and traditional clay-based products through an easy-to-use mobile platform.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎨 Artisan Module

* Add and manage pottery products
* Update stock availability
* Edit product details
* View customer orders
* Maintain artisan profile

### 🛍️ Customer Module

* Browse pottery collections
* Search and filter products
* Save favorite products
* Add products to cart
* QR-code-based payment system
* Share products via WhatsApp

### 🛠️ Admin Module

* Manage artisan details
* Monitor application statistics
* Track orders and revenue
* View product stock information

### 🌐 Additional Features

* Modern Material 3 UI design

* Eco-benefits & health-benefits information

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional pottery artisans often face challenges such as limited market access, lack of digital presence, and dependency on middlemen. Customers also struggle to discover authentic handmade clay products. Kumbara-Kala solves this problem by creating a digital marketplace that directly connects artisans with customers while preserving traditional craftsmanship and promoting sustainable products.
